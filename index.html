1. Introduction

Point4 ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes DutyArc, an Android application designed to help users manage duty schedules, shift patterns, leave, extra duty, diary/notes, calendar information, and related features.

We respect user privacy and aim to keep personal app content primarily on the user's device. This Privacy Policy explains what information DutyArc accesses or processes, how it is used, when information may be transmitted to third-party services, and the choices available to users.

2. Information Stored Locally

DutyArc stores most user-created information locally on the user's device. This may include:

  • Shift patterns and shift history
  • Manual shift changes
  • Leave and Sick Leave records
  • Extra Duty records
  • Diary and date notes
  • Custom holidays
  • Shift colors and app preferences
  • GPS Camera photos and videos
  • Local backup files created by the user

DutyArc does not automatically upload this user-created content to a Point4 or DutyArc server. DutyArc currently operates no proprietary backend server for storing this information.

3. Location Information

DutyArc's GPS Camera feature uses device location to provide location-related information for captured photos and videos.

Location information may be used to:

  • Obtain the device's current location
  • Display coordinates and location information
  • Create location stamps on photos and videos
  • Write location information to supported photo metadata
  • Obtain address/location text using the Android System Geocoder
  • Determine the OpenStreetMap tile needed for a map thumbnail

DutyArc does not send location coordinates to a server operated by Point4 or DutyArc.

However, DutyArc uses the Android System Geocoder to convert coordinates into readable address information. Depending on the device and Android system implementation, this service may use network-based system services to process location coordinates.

DutyArc also requests OpenStreetMap map tiles using geographically derived tile coordinates. Raw decimal GPS latitude and longitude are not included in the OpenStreetMap tile request used by DutyArc.

4. Camera, Photos and Videos

DutyArc requests camera access for its GPS Camera feature.

Photos and videos captured through DutyArc are stored locally in the application's storage. DutyArc does not automatically upload these photos or videos to Point4, Firebase, OpenStreetMap, or another cloud storage service.

Users may choose to export or share their media using Android's available sharing or storage features. Such user-initiated sharing is controlled by the user and the selected destination application or service.

5. Microphone

DutyArc may request microphone access when recording video with audio. Audio recorded during video capture is stored as part of the locally saved video file. DutyArc does not automatically upload these recordings.

6. Calendar Access

DutyArc may access calendar information available through Android's calendar system to display relevant calendar events alongside duty information.

Calendar information is read as needed for this functionality. DutyArc does not automatically upload calendar events to Point4 servers.

7. OpenStreetMap

DutyArc uses OpenStreetMap to download static map tiles used with GPS Camera location information.

Requests to OpenStreetMap contain map tile coordinates (zoom level and tile X/Y values) required to retrieve the relevant map image. These values are derived from geographic location and therefore correspond to an approximate geographic area.

DutyArc does not include Diary/Notes, shift information, Extra Duty information, address text, photos, or videos in these map-tile requests.

8. Firebase Crashlytics

DutyArc uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to help identify crashes and improve application stability.

When a crash occurs, Crashlytics may receive technical diagnostic information such as:

  • Crash and exception stack traces
  • App version/build information
  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Android operating system version
  • Technical device information
  • An app-installation identifier used by Firebase

DutyArc does not intentionally attach GPS coordinates, Diary/Notes content, shift information, Extra Duty records, photos, videos, names, or email addresses as custom Crashlytics data.

DutyArc currently does not include Firebase Analytics and does not include third-party advertising SDKs.

9. Backup and Restore

DutyArc currently provides local backup and restore functionality.

Backups created through DutyArc are encrypted and saved to a location selected by the user through Android's storage system. Current backup functionality does not automatically upload backups to Google Drive or another cloud service.

Google Sign-In and Google Drive cloud backup are not currently implemented in DutyArc.

10. Advertising and Analytics

DutyArc currently does not contain third-party advertising SDKs and does not currently use Firebase Analytics or another analytics SDK for tracking how users interact with the application.

Firebase Crashlytics is used specifically for crash diagnostics and application stability.

11. Data Sharing and Sale

Point4 does not sell or rent users' DutyArc data.

Limited technical information may be processed by third-party services required for specific functionality, including:

  • Google Firebase Crashlytics — crash diagnostics and stability
  • OpenStreetMap — map tile retrieval
  • Android System Geocoder/system location services — converting geographic coordinates into readable location information

The user's locally stored DutyArc content is not automatically uploaded to a Point4 backend server.

12. Data Security

DutyArc is designed so that core user-created information is primarily stored locally on the device. Local backup files created through DutyArc are encrypted using the application's backup encryption mechanism.

No device, operating system, storage method, or internet transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored DutyArc data remains on the device until it is deleted by the user, removed through available app functionality, or removed when application data is cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to Android's storage behavior.

Media or backup files that the user has separately exported outside DutyArc's private application storage may remain in the selected external location after DutyArc is uninstalled and must be managed separately by the user.

14. Children's Privacy

DutyArc is a general-purpose duty scheduling and productivity application and is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

DutyArc does not intentionally request users to provide personal profile information such as their name, date of birth, or age through an account-registration system.

15. Permissions

Depending on the features used and Android version, DutyArc may request:

  • Camera — for taking photos and recording videos
  • Microphone — for recording audio with videos
  • Location — for GPS Camera and location-stamp functionality
  • Calendar — for displaying relevant device calendar information

Certain storage-related access may also be used where required by Android versions and user-selected import/export operations.

Users may manage applicable permissions through Android system settings. Some DutyArc features may not work correctly if required permissions are denied.

16. Internet Connectivity

DutyArc is designed to keep its main scheduling and user-created content locally on the device, but the application is not completely offline.

Internet connectivity may be used for:

  • OpenStreetMap map tile downloads
  • Android/system reverse-geocoding services where applicable
  • Firebase Crashlytics diagnostic reporting

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Point4 may update this Privacy Policy when DutyArc's functionality, data practices, third-party services, or legal requirements change.

When the policy is updated, the effective date shown at the top of this Privacy Policy will be revised.
